# D1 Project proposal

## Project title
Mobile Crane Services Website and Enquiry Platform

## Executive summary
This project is a front-end website for a mobile crane services business in Madina, Ghana. The current application presents the service offering, business location, contact channels, and WhatsApp-based enquiry flow. The system is intended to help the business advertise services and collect service requests from prospective clients.

## Client and user context
- Client: Mobile crane services business in Madina, Ghana
- Primary user groups: construction managers, contractors, site managers, equipment relocation clients, and general service enquirers
- Business need: present reliable service offerings and enable quick enquiry and response via WhatsApp or phone
- Needs client confirmation: official business name, exact brand identity, approval for images, service area, and quote process

## Scope
In scope for the current repository:
- responsive landing page
- service overview and service cards
- WhatsApp enquiries
- contact details and quick enquiry form
- gallery and brand styling

Out of scope for this version:
- booking engine
- authentication
- admin dashboard
- database or CRM integration
- payment processing

## Preliminary requirements
### Functional requirements
- the website should display key crane service offerings
- users should be able to navigate to service sections and contact details
- users can send a WhatsApp message using the correct number
- the app should collect enquiry details from a quick form before redirecting to WhatsApp

### Non-functional requirements
- responsive design for mobile and desktop layouts
- accessible focus states and text contrast
- reduced-motion consideration
- production build stability

## Business and risk considerations
- Risk: business details may change during client confirmation
- Risk: service area and budget details are not yet formally confirmed
- Mitigation: mark all unconfirmed claims clearly as NEEDS CLIENT CONFIRMATION and avoid inventing official details
