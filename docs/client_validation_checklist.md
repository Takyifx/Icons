# Client validation checklist

This file records the minimum questions that must be confirmed with the real client before the project is treated as complete and lecturer-ready.

## Required validation questions
1. Official business name and approved contact details.
2. Confirmation of the seven listed services: heavy load lifting, construction work, loading and unloading, heavy equipment relocation, road and bridge construction, recovery operations, and equipment installation.
3. Existing customer enquiry workflow and whether WhatsApp is the primary contact channel.
4. Required information before quoting a crane job.
5. Actual service availability, operating constraints, and service area.
6. Approved business photographs and image usage permission.
7. Client feedback on the current website.
8. Features accepted, rejected, or requested for future versions.
9. Outstanding questions and the responsible team member for follow-up.
